### Cadence Retention under Fatigue
- When fatigue sets in during fast running, the natural failure mode is cadence collapsing while stride lengthens (braking impact).
- **Correct cue:** Let stride length naturally shorten (0.95–1.00m) while consciously maintaining turnover (160–164+ SPM).
