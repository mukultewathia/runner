## Core Training Principles

1. **Polarized Intensity Distribution (80/20):** Approximately 80% of weekly running volume in Zone 1–2 (conversational, $\le$152 BPM). ~20% in Zone 4 (Cruise intervals and threshold tempos).
2. **Individualized Chassis-First Progression:** Cap weekly volume growth gradually; peak at 32–34 km/week to protect the 93 kg frame and lower calves/Achilles from overuse.
3. **Consolidation Deloads:** Schedule a 20–25% volume reduction every 3–4 weeks (Week 16 scheduled deload) to facilitate connective tissue remodeling.
4. **Cadence & Mechanics (Observed Functional Ranges):**
   - **Fast Running ($\le$ 6:00/km):** Aim for **160–166+ SPM** turnover. When fatigue hits, shorten stride while keeping turnover active.
   - **Easy Running ($\ge$ 8:25/km):** Natural self-selected **150–156 SPM** cadence to protect joints.
5. **Running-Specific Gym Integration:** Maintain 4–5 days of lifting, but isolate heavy lower-body sessions (seated calf raises, Bulgarian split squats, single-leg RDLs) $\ge$48 hours away from long runs and quality speed workouts.
6. **Intermediate Certification Strategy:** Treat the BMM 10K as an official qualification cruise and fueling dry run, not a max-effort race.
