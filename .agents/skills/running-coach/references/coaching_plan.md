## Core Training Principles

1. **Polarized Intensity Distribution (80/20):** Approximately 80% of weekly running volume in Zone 1–2 ($\le$150 BPM, conversational). ~20% in Zone 4 (Cruise intervals and threshold tempos).
2. **Individualized Volume Progression:** Progress volume gradually (~2–3 km/week) based on tissue tolerance, lifting load, and symptom history, rather than rigid dogmatic cutoffs.
3. **Consolidation Deloads:** Schedule a 20–30% volume reduction every 3–4 weeks to facilitate connective tissue adaptation and glycogen supercompensation.
4. **Cadence & Mechanics (Observed Functional Ranges):**
   - **Fast Running ($\le$ 6:00/km):** Aim for **160–166+ SPM** turnover. When fatigue hits, let stride shorten while keeping turnover active.
   - **Easy Running ($\ge$ 8:30/km):** Natural self-selected **150–156 SPM** cadence to protect joints.
5. **Running-Specific Gym Integration:** Maintain 4–5 days of lifting, but isolate heavy lower-body sessions (seated calf raises, Bulgarian split squats, single-leg RDLs) $\ge$48 hours away from long runs and quality speed workouts.
6. **Travel & Non-Exercise Activity Integration:** Treat high walking/hiking days (sightseeing, mountain trekking) as Zone 1 endurance and structural loading rather than forcing daily runs.
