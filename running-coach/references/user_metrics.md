### Biomechanics Golden Ratio
> **Established: July 25, 2026** from 5K Time Trial lap data.
> **164 SPM at 1.03m stride** = optimal formula. Validated in Laps 1 & 2 of the time trial before mechanical breakdown.

### Death Shuffle Immunity Protocol
When fatigue hits late in a race or workout:
- ❌ Natural response: Cadence drops to 154 SPM, stride shrinks to 0.95m, pace collapses.
- ✅ Required response: **Let stride shrink (0.95–1.00m is fine), but force cadence to stay at 164 SPM.**
- Rationale: Cadence maintenance preserves momentum without requiring power from fatigued glycogen stores.
