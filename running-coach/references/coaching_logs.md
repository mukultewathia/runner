## LOG-006 | July 25, 2026 — 5K Time Trial Autopsy

**Session:** Month 2 Baseline 5K Time Trial
**Result:** 30:45 (6:09/km) — Positive split collapse
**Goal:** Sub-30:00 with negative split strategy

### Executive Summary
The athlete lined up with a disciplined negative split strategy (6:00/km → 5:45/km) and instead experienced a violent positive split, hemorrhaging nearly a full minute of pace by Lap 5. The failure was caused by **one specific, highly correctable error: pacing arrogance in Lap 1.**

The athlete did not fail because of lacking fitness. Running ~15 minutes at a 195 BPM redline at 95 kg is clinically extraordinary. The miss on sub-30:00 (finishing ~30:50) was entirely due to crossing the Lactate Threshold in minute 6 of a 30-minute race.

**Bright spots:**
- Biomechanical Golden Ratio (164 SPM / 1.03m) held perfectly in Laps 1–2.
- Cardiovascular pain tolerance is elite-level.
- Mental fortitude to finish despite complete glycogen depletion.

### Lap-by-Lap Telemetry & Coach Verdict

| Phase | Lap | Pace | Avg HR | Max HR | Cadence | Stride | Coach's Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Trap** | Lap 1 (0–1km) | 5:54/km | 165 | 180 | 166 SPM | 1.03m | Ignored 6:00/km strict limit by 6 seconds. Adrenaline masked effort. This 6-second error detonated the remaining 4km. |
| **The Debt** | Lap 2 (1–2km) | 5:55/km | 184 | 189 | 164 SPM | 1.03m | Form is perfect — biomechanics Golden Ratio maintained. But HR skyrockets 19 BPM at identical pace. Full cardiac drift. Now fully anaerobic and borrowing time. |
| **The Fracture** | Lap 3 (2–3km) | 5:53/km | 191 | 196 | 162 SPM | 1.05m | Ego response. Stride balloons to 1.05m — trying to pull the road rather than spin the cadence. At 95 kg, this massively drains glycogen. Cadence breaks below 164 for the first time. |
| **The Wall** | Lap 4 (3–4km) | 6:17/km | 195 | 198 | 158 SPM | 1.01m | Lactate flood. CNS panic. Pace drops 24 sec/km. Cadence collapses. The body begins rationing. |
| **The Collapse** | Lap 5 (4–5km) | 6:49/km | 193 | 196 | 154 SPM | 0.95m | The Death Shuffle. Glycogen gone. Pure mental grit keeping legs moving. 56 sec/km lost from best lap. |

### Key Sports Science Findings
1. **Cardiac Drift Reality:** A 6-second pacing error (5:54 vs 6:00) triggered HR to jump from 165→184 BPM between identical laps. Lactate Threshold (~170 BPM) was crossed at minute 6 instead of minute 18.
2. **Biomechanical Fracture:** Lap 3 cadence dropped to 162 SPM with stride ballooning to 1.05m — the exact overstride pattern that causes Achilles overload.
3. **198 BPM Max:** Hitting 198 BPM (96% of tested max) in Lap 4 proves elite pain tolerance. But pain tolerance is not a pacing strategy.

### Forward Plan
- **Immediate (Month 3, Weeks 1–4):** No threshold work. 80% of volume must be strict Zone 2 (≤ 150 BPM). Focus on aerobic engine expansion.
- **Cadence Mandate:** 164 SPM on every run regardless of pace or effort.
- **Next Time Trial:** August target is sub-29:00 (or sub-28:30 per macrocycle) with strict pacing discipline (6:00/km flat for first 3km, negative split in final 2km).
