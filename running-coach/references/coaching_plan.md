## Training Principles Governing All Phases

1. **80/20 Rule:** 80% of weekly volume in Zone 1–2 (≤150 BPM conversational). 20% in Zone 4–5 (intervals/tempo).
2. **10% Volume Progression:** Never increase weekly mileage or long run distance by more than 10% week-over-week.
3. **Deload Every 4th Week:** Reduce volume by 20–30% to consolidate adaptation and prevent overuse injury.
4. **164+ SPM Non-Negotiable:** Every run, every pace. Cadence is the most important mechanical variable.
5. **Structural Caps Until October 2026:** LSD runs capped at 10 km. Engine is ahead of chassis — protect the tendons.
6. **Gym Integration:** 5-day lifting program continues. Separate heavy leg days from interval/LSD days by 48+ hours.

## Race Pacing Strategy (Sub-20 5K — Target Feb 2027)

**Splits:** 4:00 / 4:00 / 4:00 / 4:00 / 3:59 = **19:59**

**Pacing Plan:**
- **km 1:** 4:05/km (feel easy, establish cadence at 170+ SPM)
- **km 2:** 4:02/km (slight increase, still controlled)
- **km 3:** 4:00/km (race pace locked in, cadence paramount)
- **km 4:** 3:58/km (commit — this is where most runners fade)
- **km 5:** 3:50/km (empty the tank — the engine must be fit enough to accelerate here)

> **Cardinal Rule:** If the first kilometer feels comfortable, the pacing is right. If it feels easy, check cadence. If it feels hard, slow down immediately — no exceptions.
